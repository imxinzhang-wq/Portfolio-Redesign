
## YouTube Shopping: Tagging in Description
**Role:** Design Lead | **Timeline:** 2024
**Team:** 1 Designer, 1 Researcher, 1 Product Manager
### Overview
I led the design strategy for "Tagging in Description," a workflow-integrated solution that transformed how YouTube creators tag products. By moving the entry point directly into the description box and implementing automated link parsing, we shifted the experience from a high-friction manual task to an intuitive part of the creation process. The final solution now drives **7% of daily GMV**.
### The Problem: Workflow Disconnect
Despite the mission to help viewers discover products from trusted creators, our existing tools were underutilized due to significant friction:
 * **Forced Search vs. Direct Links:** While creators naturally use direct product links, our tool forced them to manually search for items they already had links for. **42% of creators** cited "high perceived effort" as the reason they didn't tag.
 * **Out of Sight, Out of Mind:** The tagging tool was buried outside the primary metadata workflow. **48% of creators** reported simply forgetting the tool existed because it wasn't visible while they were writing their video descriptions.
### Strategic Approach & Design Iterations
The objective was to align the tagging feature with the creator’s natural muscle memory—writing descriptions. I explored two primary directions through rapid prototyping and user research:
 * **Direction 1: Product Chips**
   * **Concept:** Replace URLs with clean, modern product chips within the text.
   * **Findings:** While creators appreciated the aesthetics and the ability to verify trust, there were concerns regarding how viewers would interpret the chips and a lack of obvious feedback when tagging occurred.
 * **Direction 2: Product Thumbnails (Final Direction)**
   * **Concept:** Automatically parse links in the description to populate a separate, lightweight tagged product section below the text.
   * **Findings:** This was the most successful model. Creators noticed the product count immediately and felt the process was "clean" and "easy" because it eliminated the need for disruptive pop-up windows.
### Leadership & Execution
I navigated complex technical and organizational barriers to deliver this solution:
 * **Bridging the PM Gap:** I stepped in to lead product direction and milestones during the Product Manager's leave, working directly with engineering to ensure feasible delivery.
 * **Negotiating High-Traffic UI:** I convinced the YouTube Studio team to allow a new entry point in the description box—one of the most mature and high-traffic areas of the platform—by demonstrating how this pattern could scale to other features like auto-chapters and location tags.
 * **Navigating Constraints:** I led the team through severe UI and engineering constraints by iterating on a "mini-search" panel that could exist within the established Studio architecture without requiring a full redesign.
### Impact & Looking Forward
The launch proved that moving tools into the user's natural workflow is a primary driver for adoption:
 * **Business Impact:** The toolbar (excluding the manual picker) now drives a significant **7% of daily GMV**.
 * **User Validation:** Qualitative feedback was overwhelmingly positive, with creators noting that the feature is "much more convenient" and "discoverable" compared to previous areas.
 * **Scaling the Pattern:** The success of this integrated entry point provides a blueprint for future AI-driven features, such as automated timestamps and text styling.

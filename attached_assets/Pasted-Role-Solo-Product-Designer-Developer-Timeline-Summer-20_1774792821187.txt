Role: Solo Product Designer & Developer
Timeline: Summer 2025
Tools: Gemini, ChatGPT, Lovable, Replit, Codex

⸻

Overview

I built and launched an IBS tracking app from 0→1 using an AI-assisted workflow.

The goal was to explore how far a single designer could go from idea to launch by leveraging AI across research, design, and development. Instead of building a hypothetical concept, I focused on a real problem—managing my own Irritable Bowel Syndrome (IBS)—to ensure the product was grounded in actual use.



Product Direction

The app is designed to help users move beyond simple tracking toward pattern awareness.

Key goals:
	•	Make daily logging lightweight and sustainable
	•	Capture food, symptoms, and context in a structured way
	•	Enable users to reflect on patterns over time rather than rely on immediate conclusions


Approach

This project prioritized speed and iteration over extensive upfront design.

Rather than defining a fully polished product strategy before building, I focused on:
	•	getting a functional version into users’ hands early
	•	iterating based on usage and feedback
	•	using AI to reduce the cost of execution

The objective was to validate both the product idea and an AI-assisted workflow for building end-to-end products.


AI-Assisted Workflow

AI was used as a supporting layer throughout the product lifecycle:

Research & synthesis
Used language models to summarize discussions from Reddit and App Store reviews, helping identify common pain points and gaps in existing solutions.

Scoping & structure
Generated an initial PRD and information architecture to define scope and core flows. These were refined manually to ensure the product remained focused and buildable.

Design & iteration
Skipped traditional high-fidelity prototyping. Used quick sketches and AI-generated UI as a starting point, then manually adjusted layout, spacing, and visual details.

Development
Used AI tools for scaffolding and debugging. A large portion of the application was generated and iterated quickly, while more complex issues required manual intervention and fixes.

AI helped accelerate execution, while final decisions and quality control remained manual.

Outcome
	•	Launched a fully functional app from 0→1
	•	2,000+ downloads in the first month
	•	4.9 average rating
	•	Positive early feedback on usability and clarity

⸻

Reflection

This project demonstrated that AI can significantly reduce the time required to move from idea to shipped product, especially for solo-led work.

At the same time, it reinforced that AI is most effective when paired with human judgment:
	•	AI is useful for generating a baseline and accelerating iteration
	•	Human input is still required for scope control, refinement, and final quality

The role of the designer in this workflow shifts toward:
	•	framing problems clearly
	•	guiding structure and direction
	•	making final decisions on quality and usability


This project is less about a single product, and more about testing a practical way of building—where AI supports execution, while human judgment defines the outcome.
